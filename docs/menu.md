# Cledis Burgers & Beer — Menu (source of truth)

Transcribed from the official menu boards on **cledis.com** (the in-store / website menu),
which is the authoritative source for what the kitchen actually serves. Third-party
delivery apps (DoorDash / Uber Eats / aggregators) show a different, smaller subset and
should **not** be used as the source of truth.

**Sources**
- Elm Hill food menu: https://www.cledis.com/elm-hill-food-menu — images `elmhill26page1.png`, `elmhill26page2.png` (2026 menu)
- Bellevue food menu: https://www.cledis.com/bellevue-menu — images `Bellevue UPDATED 5-15-25 Page 1/2`
- Gulch food menu: https://www.cledis.com/gulch-menu

**Cross-location note:** The menu is essentially identical across Elm Hill, Bellevue, and Gulch.
The only differences observed: the Elm Hill 2026 board trims **Hoosier Daddy** and **Bleu Berry Melt**
from Sandwiches and does not show the **Kids** section; the Bellevue board includes all three.
The inventory seed carries the full union (all 44 items) at every location.

> All items served à la carte unless otherwise noted.
